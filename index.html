I am an undergraduate at POSTECH, currently a research intern at the Data Systems Lab under Prof. Wook-Shin Han.

Most of what I build starts from a personal pain point — something tedious I had to do by hand, or a workflow that felt like it should already be automated. Assignment deadlines that never made it into my calendar, lecture recordings I was never going to revisit, school mail I kept sorting manually. I like the gap between “this is annoying” and “this is fixed.”

Lately the same instinct has pointed at language-model agents: they do a great deal in the middle of a task, and almost none of it can be checked. That is what I work on now.

Research

  • PROVE: Provenance for Retrieval via Observable, Verified Evidence 2026.06 – present

    Multi-hop QA agents hold their working memory as a free-form blob, so nothing they conclude mid-search can be checked against the evidence behind it. PROVE puts a verifiable scratchpad next to free-form reasoning — think in prose, answer through the scratchpad — and asks whether that yields intermediate reward usable for RL without costing accuracy. Currently moving from evaluation to RL training.

  • Semantic and Graph Context for Personal Knowledge Bases 2026 – present

    Independent work on serving a personal note graph to language-model agents: local embedding search combined with link structure, exposed through the Model Context Protocol under an explicit write guard. Released as obsidian-everywhere.
